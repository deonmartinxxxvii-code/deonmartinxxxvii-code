# Deon Martin

I'm a Salesforce Administrator and Platform App Builder with a background in healthcare operations and regulated healthcare environments.

Before moving into Salesforce, I worked in hospital pharmacy, sterile compounding, pharmaceutical manufacturing support, inventory management, and other regulated healthcare settings. Those experiences taught me the importance of reliable workflows, accountability, and clear communication when people depend on a system to do the right thing.

When I transitioned into Salesforce, I wanted to build something that reflected those experiences instead of another sample CRM project. That's how CareConnect started.

---

## Featured Project

### CareConnect

CareConnect is a Salesforce application I designed to improve hospital operations by bringing patient movement, transportation, department coordination, and operational work into one platform.

Building CareConnect has been my way of learning Salesforce by solving real business problems. It has given me hands-on experience with data modeling, security, automation, reporting, Apex, Lightning Web Components, and user experience design.
